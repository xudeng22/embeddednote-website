# About EmbeddedNote

EmbeddedNote is a bilingual engineering journal focusing on embedded systems, semiconductor devices, practical hardware design, and open-source engineering.

## What we publish

We examine projects from schematics and source code to real-world implementation. We favor reproducible experiments, precise attribution, and useful engineering judgment.
