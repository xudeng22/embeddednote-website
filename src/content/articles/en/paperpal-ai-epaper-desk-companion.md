It's not meant to replace your computer, your phone, or your smart speaker.

It has one small but useful job: turn things you don't want to forget—or keep putting off—into an e-paper card that stays visible on your desk, and remind you when it matters.

The current iOS and Android apps support both English and Chinese, including voice input.
