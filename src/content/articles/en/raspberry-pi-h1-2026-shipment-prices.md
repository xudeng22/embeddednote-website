Raspberry Pi's June 2026 trading update offered two useful numbers and one important qualification. In its **June 5 announcement**, the company *expected* first-half shipments to exceed **4 million units** and adjusted EBITDA to reach at least **$38 million**. These were forecasts for the six months ending June 30, not yet published actual results. <a href="https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/trading-update-tik9c69fr16wj1q.html">The original exchange announcement</a> is the appropriate source for those claims.

The company cited unit growth, a more favorable product mix, and the use of DRAM inventory bought in the preceding year. This matters because a higher board price alone does not explain a company's profit. Inventory purchase costs, product configurations, customer mix and volumes all affect the result.

![Raspberry Pi H1 2026 trading-update illustration](/uploads/raspberry-pi-h1-2026-shipment-prices/raspberry-pi-h1-2026-shipment-prices-01.webp)

## What happened after the June forecast?

**Update — November 6, 2026:** Raspberry Pi released its H1 2026 interim results on September 24. Actual unit shipments were **4.2 million**, revenue **$256.9 million**, and adjusted EBITDA **$40.3 million**. These numbers supersede the June forecast; the original report was not evidence of 4.2 million shipped units. Adjusted EBITDA is a company-defined performance measure and should not be confused with net profit. [Read the H1 interim-results announcement](https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/RPI/interim-results-e9exgc54mbhey5h.html).

| Metric | June 5 forecast | September 24 reported result |
|---|---:|---:|
| H1 unit shipments | More than 4.0 million | 4.2 million |
| H1 adjusted EBITDA | At least $38 million | $40.3 million |
| H1 revenue | Not stated in this trading-update summary | $256.9 million |

## Why the Pi 5 price needs a date

A Raspberry Pi 5 uses a Broadcom BCM2712 with four Arm Cortex-A76 cores, and its memory is soldered rather than user-upgradable. That makes memory capacity a purchasing decision, not an easy later retrofit. But a single price table becomes obsolete quickly during a DRAM shortage.

The June article referred to a historical configuration range that included a **$305** 16 GB Pi 5. On **November 2, 2026**, Raspberry Pi announced another **$80** increase for that model, bringing its list price to **$385**. The manufacturer explicitly attributed the increase to constrained 16 GB DRAM supply. Those prices refer to different dates; they should not be blended into one timeless price range. [Manufacturer announcement](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/price-rises-for-some-16gb-raspberry-pi-products/).

![Raspberry Pi pricing discussion illustration](/uploads/raspberry-pi-h1-2026-shipment-prices/raspberry-pi-h1-2026-shipment-prices-02.webp)

For a new design, benchmark the **minimum memory configuration that actually runs the workload** before comparing prices. A camera pipeline with multiple buffers, a browser kiosk and a headless gateway have very different memory requirements. Include power, storage, cooling, connectors, software support and supply continuity in total system cost. A cheaper sticker price on a different SBC is not necessarily a cheaper maintained product.
