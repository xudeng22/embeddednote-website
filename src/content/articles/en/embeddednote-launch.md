Welcome to **EmbeddedNote**, a bilingual engineering journal focused on practical embedded development.

Our goal is to turn complex technologies into engineering knowledge that can be understood, tested, and applied.

## Technical Coverage

EmbeddedNote covers six major areas:

- **Open Source Projects:** GitHub projects, schematics, BOMs, and implementation details.
- **MCU & SoC:** STM32, ESP32, RISC-V, and other embedded platforms.
- **Hardware Design:** PCB design, power management, and circuit interfaces.
- **Connectivity:** BLE, Wi-Fi, NFC, CAN, and other communication technologies.
- **Display Technology:** E-paper, TFT, and Mini LED technologies.
- **Development Tools:** Debugging, simulation, IDEs, and testing equipment.

## Editorial Principles

We focus on technical accuracy, engineering practice, reproducible methods, and clear source attribution.

Where third-party projects or materials are referenced, their original sources and applicable license terms will be identified.
