A series of reported price increases from major semiconductor vendors has put component costs back in the spotlight. Is the industry moving into another upswing in the semiconductor cycle?

**NXP:** A price-adjustment letter circulating in the market, purportedly from NXP, says that selected products would be repriced from April 1, 2026. The source document does not independently authenticate the letter, so the claim should be treated as a reported notice rather than a company-verified announcement.

![Price change letter purportedly issued by NXP in March 2026](/uploads/semiconductor-price-increases-2026/semiconductor-price-increases-2026-01.webp)

**Texas Instruments:** Supply-chain reports in March 2026 pointed to another round of TI price adjustments effective April 1. The reports cited possible increases of **15% to 85%** for certain product families, including digital isolators and power-management ICs. That range should not be read as a uniform increase across TI's entire catalog or every customer's orders.

The same reports also referenced earlier adjustments of roughly 10% to 30% for parts used in industrial and automotive applications. The actual impact on a bill of materials depends on the specific part number, contract and distribution channel.

![Texas Instruments pricing update notice in Chinese](/uploads/semiconductor-price-increases-2026/semiconductor-price-increases-2026-02.webp)

**Analog Devices (ADI):** An earlier customer notice indicated that pricing changes would take effect on February 1, 2026, across affected products.

Rather than applying one flat percentage to everything, the reported adjustments vary by product and customer. Industry sources cited increases of about **10%–15%** for commercial-grade parts and around **15%** for some industrial-grade devices. Nearly 1,000 military-specification part numbers carrying the **/883** suffix were reported to face increases of up to **30%**. These are reported ranges, not a substitute for a current manufacturer or distributor quotation.

![Analog Devices distributor pricing notice dated December 2025](/uploads/semiconductor-price-increases-2026/semiconductor-price-increases-2026-03.webp)

**Reporting note:** Price ranges above were published in industry and supply-chain coverage; they should not be interpreted as official list prices for every part number. For a purchase decision, confirm current quotes with the manufacturer or authorized distributor.
