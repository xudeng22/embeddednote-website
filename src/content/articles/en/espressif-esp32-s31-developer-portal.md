Espressif has refreshed its bilingual ESP32-S31 website and developer resources. The company's ESP32-S31 SoC brings a dual-core RISC-V processor together with several wireless standards and high-bandwidth peripheral interfaces.

![Espressif ESP32-S31 processor promotional image](/uploads/espressif-esp32-s31-developer-portal/espressif-esp32-s31-developer-portal-01.webp)

**Chip family:** ESP32-S31

**CPU:** Dual-core, 32-bit high-performance RISC-V processor running at up to 320 MHz

**Memory:** 512 KB of on-chip SRAM, with support for external 8-bit DDR PSRAM at up to 250 MHz

**Wireless connectivity:** 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE 5.4, Bluetooth Classic, Thread 1.4 and Zigbee 3.0

**Interfaces and accelerators:** 16-bit DVP camera interface, 24-bit RGB display interface, USB 2.0 High-Speed OTG, Pixel Processing Accelerator (PPA), JPEG codec and 1 Gbps Ethernet MAC

**Target applications:** Multimedia and edge-AI devices, LE Audio products, IoT endpoints, gigabit gateways, connected appliances, voice-controlled devices, smart-home hubs and video doorbells.

Espressif positions the ESP32-S31 for edge AI and machine-learning workloads, including neural-network inference, digital signal processing, computer vision and intelligent audio. The idea is to combine those workloads with the connectivity and efficiency expected of an embedded SoC.

The dedicated site is available in English and Simplified Chinese. Beyond specifications, it brings together hardware documentation, development-board information and application examples—useful starting points for anyone evaluating the chip.

**English developer site:** https://esp32-s31.espressif.com/en

**Simplified Chinese developer site:** https://esp32-s31.espressif.com/zh-hans
